By checking the acceptance box, paying a fee or deposit, approving a proposal or invoice, or otherwise authorizing us to begin, you ("Client," "you," or "your") agree to these Terms.

1. Our Services

BP Design Group provides residential interior design and space-planning services, including consultations, design plans, product and finish selections, renderings, sourcing, and related design support.

We are a design firm—not a contractor, architect, engineer, plumber, electrician, structural professional, or building-code official. We do not perform or supervise construction, demolition, installation, trade work, inspections, or permitting.

You hire and pay your own contractors and other professionals. They are responsible for construction methods, field conditions, structural requirements, permits, code compliance, safety, installation, workmanship, and final verification of the project.

Our plans and renderings communicate design intent only. They are not construction, engineering, permit, shop, or fabrication drawings.

2. Design Packages

One Room — $250
Includes one in-home consultation, one phone consultation after the home visit, one design plan and rendering, and one revision round.

Two Rooms — $500
Includes one in-home consultation, one phone consultation after the home visit, design plans and renderings for two rooms, and two revision rounds.

Three or More Rooms — $250 per room
Includes one in-home consultation, one phone consultation after the home visit, a design plan and rendering for each selected room, and two revision rounds.

Three-room packages ($750) are paid in full when booked. Projects of four or more rooms require a $1,000 deposit, applied toward the package total, with the balance due when the designs are delivered. Projects of more than ten rooms are quoted individually.

A "revision round" means one consolidated set of requested changes. A new design direction, additional room, substantial scope change, or work resulting from previously undisclosed conditions is additional work.

We generally aim to deliver the initial design within three business days after the home visit. This is an estimate, not a guaranteed completion date, and may be extended while we are waiting for information, decisions, approvals, access, or circumstances outside our reasonable control.

3. Additional Services & Payment

Additional revisions are $200 each. Additional consultations and other design services are $300 per hour.

We will tell you about additional charges and obtain your approval before beginning additional billable work. Any estimate of hours or cost is an estimate unless expressly identified as a fixed price.

Hourly services require a $600 advance deposit. The deposit is applied toward authorized services and is not a separate fee or minimum charge. Any unused amount will be refunded after services end and all outstanding charges are satisfied.

If charges exceed the deposit, we will invoice the balance. Invoices are due within 24 hours of delivery unless otherwise stated. We may pause work on overdue accounts.

Payments made by ACH or bank transfer may take several business days to clear, and we may wait for cleared payment before beginning or continuing services.

4. Cancellations & Rescheduling

Cancel at least 48 hours before your home visit for a full refund.

Cancellations made less than 48 hours before the visit, missed appointments, and cancellations after the home visit are non-refundable.

You may reschedule once. There is no fee with at least 24 hours' notice. Less than 24 hours' notice may result in a $250 rescheduling fee. Further rescheduling may require an additional fee or new booking.

If hourly services are cancelled after work begins, you are responsible for services already performed and authorized non-cancellable costs. Any unused deposit will be refunded.

Nothing in these Terms limits a refund or cancellation right that cannot legally be waived.
