1. Our Services

BP Design Group provides residential interior design and space-planning services, including consultations, design concepts and plans, finish and fixture selections, product recommendations, renderings, sourcing assistance, and related design support.

BP Design Group is a design firm and is not a general contractor, construction contractor, architect, engineer, plumber, electrician, structural professional, or building-code official unless expressly stated otherwise in a separate written agreement.

We do not perform, direct, control, or supervise construction, demolition, installation, plumbing, electrical, structural, mechanical, or other trade work. We do not obtain permits or inspect construction work.

You are responsible for selecting, hiring, supervising, and paying your contractors, installers, suppliers, and other professionals.

Your contractor and other applicable licensed professionals are responsible for determining construction methods, field conditions, structural requirements, permitting requirements, code compliance, safety requirements, and the proper installation of all materials and products.

Our design plans and renderings communicate design intent and are not construction documents, engineering drawings, permit drawings, shop drawings, or fabrication drawings unless we expressly agree otherwise in writing.

2. Design Packages

One-Room Package — $250

The One-Room Package includes:

  • One in-home visit and design consultation;
  • A design plan for one room; and
  • One round of reasonable revisions to the initial design plan.

Two-Room Package — $500

The Two-Room Package includes:

  • One in-home visit and design consultation;
  • Design plans for two rooms; and
  • Two rounds of reasonable revisions to the initial design plans.

Multi-Room Package — $250 per room (three or more rooms)

The Multi-Room Package includes:

  • One in-home visit and design consultation;
  • Design plans for each room selected at booking; and
  • Two rounds of reasonable revisions to the initial design plans.

A three-room package ($750) is paid in full when booked. Packages of four or more rooms require a $1,000 deposit when booked. The deposit is credited to the package total, and the remaining balance is invoiced when the design plans are delivered and is due under Section 4. Projects of more than ten rooms are quoted individually.

Unless otherwise stated in writing, revisions are limited to the rounds listed for each package above.

A "revision round" means one consolidated set of requested changes submitted by the Client after reviewing a design version.

Revision rounds do not include an entirely new design concept, a material change in the agreed project direction, additional rooms, substantial changes to the project scope, or work caused by previously undisclosed conditions. Such work may be billed as additional services under Section 4.

We generally aim to deliver the initial design plan within three business days after the home visit. This is an estimated turnaround time and not a guaranteed completion date.

Delivery dates may be extended if we are waiting for Client information, selections, approvals, measurements, access, supplier information, or other matters outside our reasonable control.

3. Payment and Booking

Package fees are paid in full when booked, except for Multi-Room Packages of four or more rooms, which require the deposit described in Section 2, unless we agree otherwise in writing.

Payments may be processed through Stripe or another payment processor made available through our website. BP Design Group does not directly store full payment card numbers.

After payment is received, we will generally contact you within one business day to coordinate the home visit.

Payments made by ACH or bank transfer may require several business days to clear. We may delay scheduling, delivery, purchasing activity, or other work until funds have cleared.

You authorize us and our payment processor to process the amounts you approve in connection with your booking, proposal, invoice, or other authorized services.

4. Hourly and Additional Design Services

Services outside the scope of an included design package may include:

  • Additional revisions;
  • Additional renderings;
  • Additional rooms or spaces;
  • Product and material sourcing;
  • Vendor coordination;
  • Additional meetings or site visits;
  • Design modifications requested after approval;
  • Renovation-phase design assistance; and
  • Other requested design services.

Unless otherwise agreed in writing, additional revisions beyond those included in your package are billed at $200 per revision, and additional design consultations and other additional services are billed at $300 per hour.

We will notify you before beginning hourly work. For larger requests, we may provide an estimated number of hours or estimated cost. Unless expressly identified as a fixed fee in writing, an estimate is not a guaranteed maximum price.

Hourly services require a $600 advance deposit before work begins.

The deposit will be credited against amounts owed for hourly services and other authorized charges. Any unused portion of the deposit will be refunded after the project is completed or terminated and all amounts owed to BP Design Group have been satisfied.

If fees exceed the deposit, we will issue an invoice for the remaining balance.

Invoices are due within 24 hours after delivery unless the invoice states otherwise.

We may pause or decline further work while an account is overdue.

5. Cancellations, Rescheduling, and Refunds

Cancellation Before the Home Visit

If you cancel at least 48 hours before the scheduled home visit, the applicable package fee will be refunded in full.

Cancellation Within 48 Hours

If you cancel less than 48 hours before the scheduled home visit, or fail to attend or provide access for the scheduled appointment, the package fee is non-refundable.

Cancellation After the Home Visit

Once the home visit has occurred, design services are considered to have begun and the package fee is non-refundable.

Rescheduling

You may reschedule a home visit one time.

If you provide at least 24 hours' notice, no rescheduling fee will apply.

If you request to reschedule less than 24 hours before the appointment, a $50 rescheduling fee may apply.

Further rescheduling may require an additional fee or a new booking.

Hourly Services

If hourly services are cancelled after work begins, you are responsible for time already performed and any non-cancellable costs or commitments incurred with your authorization. Any remaining unused portion of an advance deposit will be refunded.

Approved refunds will generally be returned to the original payment method.

Nothing in this section limits any cancellation or refund right that cannot lawfully be waived.
