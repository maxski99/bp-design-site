How it works from plan to rendering

Three steps from first visit to a plan you can build.

  1. Home visit

    We walk the space, take measurements and plan the layout, cabinetry, finishes, and lighting for your dream kitchen or bath.

  2. The Design

    Our Lead Designer takes your dream space and creates a perfect visual rendering. We wait for your review and approval then can make the final revisions.

  3. Build with confidence

    Take the plans and precise renderings to your contractor with a clear picture of the finished space.
