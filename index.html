    Common questions

    Are you a contractor?

    No. BP Design Group is a design firm. We create your plans and renderings; you hire your own contractor and installers, and they verify all measurements and site conditions before ordering materials or building.

    What happens at the home visit?

    Our Lead Designer walks the space with you, takes measurements for the floor plan, and talks through how you use the room, the look you want, and what matters most to you.

    How soon will I see my design?

    For one or two rooms, we aim to deliver your designs within three to five business days after the home visit; for larger projects, we'll give you an estimate when you book. Then we review them with you by phone.

    What if I want changes?

    Each room includes one free revision. Each revision beyond that is $150.

    What does it cost?

    $300 for the first room, including the home visit, and $250 for each additional room. The full amount is paid when you book, and all fees are non-refundable.

    Can I reschedule or cancel?

    You can reschedule your home visit once with at least 48 hours' notice. With less notice, or if the visit is missed, a new $300 retainer is needed to rebook; room fees you've already paid carry over. All fees are non-refundable, but if we cancel or reschedule, you can request a full refund.

    Where do you work?

    We design kitchens and bathrooms for homes across Chicagoland and the surrounding suburbs. Beyond a reasonable distance, we may quote a travel fee.
